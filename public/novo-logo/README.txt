NOVO — LOGO FILE PACKAGE
=========================

40 files total: 20 SVG (pure vector, editable) + 20 PNG (1024x1024,
transparent background, verified — ready to upload anywhere).

FOLDER GUIDE
------------
01_Motion_N/     The recommended mark — a lowercase "n" whose right
                 stroke becomes a forward arrow. 4 colors.
02_Pin_Food/     Location pin + delivery bag combined. 4 colors.
                 (Note: the small bag detail auto-swaps to whichever
                 color contrasts best against the pin color.)
03_Double_Arrow/ Two arrows forming a loop — food out, rider back. 4 colors.
04_Wordmark/     "novo" in real Poppins Bold, outlined to permanent
                 vector paths (no font dependency — looks identical
                 everywhere, including Illustrator, Canva, Figma,
                 and print). 4 colorways, each with a matching dot.
05_Rider_N/      A bold N with a helmet riding at its peak. 4 colors.

COLOR KEY
---------
orange   #FF6B00   — primary. Works on white or dark.
black    #0F1115   — use on white or light backgrounds only.
white    #FFFFFF   — use on dark or colored backgrounds only.
green    #00C853   — secondary/alt colorway.

FILE TYPES
----------
.svg  — Pure vector. Infinitely scalable, no quality loss. Open in
        Illustrator, Figma, Inkscape, or Canva. Best choice for
        large-format printing, app icon generators, and anywhere
        that accepts vector uploads.

.png  — 1024x1024, transparent background (RGBA alpha, verified —
        not white). Best for app store listings, social media,
        websites, and any upload field that doesn't accept SVG.

APP ICON NOTE
--------------
Per the design brief, Concept 01 (Motion N) and Concept 02 (Pin +
Food) are the two strongest candidates for a small app icon (512x512
and below) — both stay legible at favicon scale. Concept 03 and 05
also hold up well; the wordmark (04) is not meant for icon use.

Need a different color, a specific size, or the app-icon-with-rounded-
square-background version? Every mark is a handful of simple shapes —
open the .svg in a text editor and edit the fill="..." hex code, or
just ask for another export.
